# MARIN★ (マリン・スター)

MARIN★ is a psychological horror visual novel that explores trauma, identity, consequences, and outliving one's life. At its heart, the story is about trauma, identity, guilt, consequences, love, and the terrifying fact that the truth can outlive the person who tries to outrun it.

Marin Watanabe, an aspiring daughter of the Watanabe family, was taken away from her mother's custody. As she tries to navigate her new life in the United States, her environment is getting increasingly hostile. Will you be able to handle Marin's circumstances and help her? Or will you dig her into a deeper hole?

The game is built on the Ren'Py engine. The game is branched, which means players are forced to make decisions and choices under pressure. Instead of a traditional third-person reading experience, the player takes on the role of the protagonist, which immerses the player in the protagonist's mind. The game leans heavily on sensory details, audio, and the player's decision-making during critical game events.

The game also contains a save system. Keep in mind, this system is really important because there are a lot of choices and branching present in the game. If you want to take an alternative route, you are recommended to save the game.
