# MARIN★ (マリン・スター)

MARIN★ is a psychological horror visual novel that explores trauma, identity, consequences, and outliving one's life. At its heart, the story is about trauma, identity, guilt, consequences, love, and the terrifying fact that the truth can outlive the person who tries to outrun it.

Marin Watanabe, an aspiring daughter of the Watanabe family, was taken away from her mother's custody. As she tries to navigate her new life in the United States, her environment is getting increasingly hostile. Will you be able to handle Marin's circumstances and help her? Or will you dig her into a deeper hole?

If you like psychological horror with a heavy contrast, this game is for you! The game features Marin Watanabe as she navigates the dark circumstances that surround her environment and threaten her own future. You, the player, control Marin Watanabe's choices and how she can navigate the world that she is in. Whether it's the canonized version of the story or alternative routes, you can influence Marin's life by making decisions. Remember, each of your decisions impacts her life in multiple aspects. Think wisely before picking a route for Marin.

The game is built on the Ren'Py engine. The game is played in first-person perspective inside the protagonist's mind, which means players are forced to make decisions and choices under pressure. Instead of a traditional third-person reading experience, the player takes on the role of the protagonist, which immerses the player in the protagonist's mind. The game leans heavily on sensory details, audio, and the player's decision-making during critical game events.

The game also contains a save system. Keep in mind, this system is really important because there are a lot of choices and branching present in the game. If you want to take an alternative route, you are recommended to save the game. Auto saves will be added later as the game develops. Auto saves will be activated in every critical moment in the gameplay, which is always in time-sensitive events and quick time events that are prompted. Be sure to save the game once you reach a critical part of the story!
