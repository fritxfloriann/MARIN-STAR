# MARIN-STAR PROJECT

MARIN★ is a psychological horror visual horror that explores trauma, identity, consequences, and outliving one's life. At its heart, the story is about trauma, identity, guilt, consequences, love, and the terrifying fact that the truth can outlive the person who tries to outrun it.

The game is built on the Ren'Py engine. The game is branched, which means players are forced to make decisions and choices under pressure. Instead of a traditional third person reading experience, the player takes on the role of the protagonist, which immerses the player into what's in mind of the protagonist.
