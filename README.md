# MARIN-STAR PROJECT

MARIN★ is a psychological horror visual novel that explores trauma, identity, consequences, and outliving one's life. At its heart, the story is about trauma, identity, guilt, consequences, love, and the terrifying fact that the truth can outlive the person who tries to outrun it.

The game is built on the Ren'Py engine. The game is branched, which means players are forced to make decisions and choices under pressure. Instead of a traditional third-person reading experience, the player takes on the role of the protagonist, which immerses the player in the protagonist's mind.

The copyright will be available once the soundtrack, character designs, dialogues, and copyright arrangements between me and illustrator are finalized. These factors matter a lot when it comes to game development, publication, and who gets monetized. Generally, the copyright will be split from person to person, as what they've contributed to MARIN★ is what they partially own. While art can be held by their own copyright, the underlying aspects and character are still owned by the author. 

Upon monetization, the payment gets split and distributed to each of the illustrators, editors, music artists, and the author. This is to compensate for their hard work, as they deserve it.
